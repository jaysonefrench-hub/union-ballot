  • Simple enough that if your secretary can run it, it's ready

Florida PERC: know the hard stop

Contract ratification in Florida is electronic only with a current PERC variance on file. Under Beightol v. United Teachers of Dade, PERC ratification is otherwise by meeting or mail ballot per FAC 60CC-4.002. Union Ballot enforces this stop — it will not let you run an electronic PERC ratification without one.

  • Union Ballot is not PERC-approved e-voting. No vendor is.
  • Union Ballot does not run representation or certification elections. PERC runs those.

Not legal advice. Have your counsel review your procedures before any binding vote.

How it works

  1. The committee opens a vote

    Decryption keys are split among keyholders you choose — one per slate, plus a neutral.

  2. Members get one-time credentials

    Voters never log in and never type their name. Paper ballot stays available.

  3. Members cast in seconds

    Enter the credential, mark the ballot, done. Choices are never echoed back.

  4. Keyholders tally together

    Observers watch the count. Records export in one click and are kept one year.
